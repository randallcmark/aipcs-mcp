# AIPCS name and logo policy

This policy explains how to refer to the AIPCS project without confusing users
about a project's origin, status, or endorsement. It does not grant or limit
any copyright permissions under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE).

## Project names and logo

The project uses the names **AIPCS** and **AIPCS MCP**, and the AIPCS logo in
this repository, as project identifiers. They are not registered trademarks at
the time this policy is published.

## Permitted factual references

You may use the names to accurately describe this project or your relationship
to it. For example:

- “based on AIPCS”;
- “a fork of AIPCS MCP”; or
- “works with AIPCS MCP”.

Those references must be truthful and must not suggest that the project
maintainer has reviewed, endorsed, certified, sponsored, or is responsible for
your work.

## Forks and derivatives

You may fork or modify the code under Apache-2.0. Use a clearly distinct name
and visual identity for a fork, derivative, package, company, domain, or social
account. Make clear that it is a fork or derivative when that context matters.

Do not use AIPCS, AIPCS MCP, or the logo as the primary branding of another
project in a way that could cause users to mistake it for the official project.

## Permission and changes

Written permission from the project maintainer is required for use that implies
official status, endorsement, sponsorship, certification, or compatibility
certification. The maintainer may update this policy for future uses; it does
not retrospectively change rights granted under Apache-2.0.
